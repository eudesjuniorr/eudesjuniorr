### 👨‍🔬 About me

I'm 28, I hold a **degree in Pharmacy**, and I'm in the middle of a deliberate **career transition into industrial automation** and related fields.

Coming from a lab-science background isn't a detour (it's how I work). I'm used to protocols, measurements, controlled variables, and writing things down properly. I've simply pointed that same method at microcontrollers, PLCs, and electronic circuits.

Everything I know about this new field, I learned **on my own**: reading, building, breaking, measuring, fixing, and documenting. This profile and my [Lab](https://github.com/eudesjuniorr) are the public logbook of that process.

### 🔧 What I'm learning right now

- **Arduino & microcontrollers** — the entry point for everything else
- **PLCs and Ladder logic** — the actual language of the factory floor
- **Electronics and circuit theory** — because automation without electronics is just guessing
- **C++ and Python** applied to control and instrumentation

### 📐 How I document my projects

Old habits from academia die hard, and this one is worth keeping. Every published project follows a paper-like structure:

1. **Motivation** — why this project exists
2. **Theoretical background** — formulas and relevant calculations
3. **Component list**
4. **Schematics**
5. **Commented source code**

Written for my future self first, and for anyone learning the same things second.
